Children's Services Award 2010 [MA000120]

This Fair Work Commission consolidated modern award incorporates all amendments up to and including 1 March 2026.

1. Title

This award is the Children's Services Award 2010.

2. Commencement and transitional

This fixture is used for deterministic acceptance testing.

3. Definitions

Ordinary hourly rate means the minimum hourly rate for the employee classification.

4. Coverage

The award covers employers and employees in children's services.

10. Part-time employees

Part-time employees work agreed regular patterns. Work outside the regular pattern may be overtime unless otherwise agreed.

13. Classifications

Classification levels identify children's services employees and support workers.

14. Minimum rates

Minimum rates include CSE Level 3 at $1121.80 per week and $29.52 per hour.

15. Allowances

Allowances include broken shift, laundry, first aid, meal, excess fares, vehicle and educational leader allowances.

18. Higher duties

Employees required to perform higher duties for at least 2 hours are paid the higher classification for that period.

21. Ordinary hours of work and rostering

Ordinary hours are worked within the ordinary span, subject to daily and weekly limits.

22. Breaks and rest periods

Employees are entitled to meal breaks, rest pauses and adequate rest between periods of work.

23. Overtime and penalty rates

Overtime is paid at time and a half for the first two hours and double time thereafter. A written agreement may convert overtime to time off instead of payment.

25. Annual leave and loading

Annual leave loading is paid on leave and compared with the applicable penalty outcome where required.

27. Public holidays

Public holidays are paid at public holiday rates and may be substituted by agreement.
